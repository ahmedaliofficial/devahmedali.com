# Service positioning research — 3 November 2026

## Decision

Expand Ahmed Ali's three broad service offers into eight clear buying options. Lead with SaaS/MVP development, practical AI, and workflow integrations; retain websites, commerce, mobile applications, desktop applications, and product improvement as distinct routes into an enquiry. The user explicitly requested websites, mobile apps, desktop apps, and automation as visible offers. The ordering is a recommendation combining marketplace evidence with Ahmed's demonstrated work, not a measured ranking of his likely revenue.

Keep marketplace statistics out of the public portfolio. Clients need to recognise their problem, see relevant work, understand the deliverable, and start a conversation. This record supports the positioning choices.

The annual Upwork research says contracted jobs while its press-release methodology says completed jobs. Both identify freelancer earnings as the measure; this record does not resolve their eligibility-wording difference or equate the figures with hires. Its ranked categories and growth categories are different views: a high growth rate does not establish high absolute demand.

September Upwork search-growth rankings require at least 50 current-month and 20 prior-year occurrences. Its contract momentum analysis uses separate thresholds. Neither source provides a census of global freelance demand, an estimate of Ahmed's addressable market, or evidence for a conversion guarantee. Search counts reflect interest rather than purchases. Percentage growth can be amplified by a small base. Platform scope, differing units, and periods make pooling or averaging these figures inappropriate.

## Presentation guidance

- Present eight short choices on the service page, with a stronger first tier for SaaS, AI, and automation. A homepage can show a compact service index without eight long explanations. Keep mobile and desktop visible as separate offers, following the user's request.
- Start each service with the client problem and a tangible result. Put framework and infrastructure names in case studies or supporting details.
- Link each service to relevant work where proof exists. Distinguish an illustrative mockup, a development build, and a released product.
- Use enquiry options matching the catalogue, with an "I'm not sure yet" option. Invite a short description of the problem rather than requiring a technical specification.
- Offer a discovery conversation, scoped build, or focused improvement engagement. Do not invent prices, delivery guarantees, testimonials, client logos, or ROI claims.
- Do not add unrelated AI video, marketing operations, social media management, or dedicated security/compliance services only because a marketplace reports growth. Strong demand elsewhere does not establish relevant delivery experience.
